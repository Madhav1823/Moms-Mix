For Punugulu:

  1. Take 1 measure of Mom's Mix Punugulu Mix.
  2. Add 1 measure of water.
  3. Add salt to taste.
  4. Add chopped onion and green chillies.
  5. Deep fry spoonfuls in hot oil until golden brown. Serve with coconut chutney.

💡 For Uttapam or Gunta Pongannalu: Add some extra water to the same batter to get a thinner, pourable consistency, then cook on a tawa or in a pongannalu pan.
