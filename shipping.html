Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about delivery timelines, charges, and coverage.

Last Updated: October 2026  ·  Ships From: Hyderabad, Telangana  ·  Coverage: Pan-India

At Mom's Mix, we take care to pack and dispatch every order promptly so your traditional breakfast premixes reach you fresh and in perfect condition.

1. Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next business day.

You will receive a WhatsApp or SMS notification once your order is packed and dispatched, along with a tracking number where applicable.

2. Delivery Timelines

Delivery Zone Estimated Delivery
Hyderabad & Secunderabad 1–2 business days
Telangana & Andhra Pradesh 2–4 business days
Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata) 3–5 business days
Rest of India 5–7 business days
Remote / Rural Areas 7–10 business days

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary during peak seasons, festivals, or due to courier delays beyond our control.

3. Shipping Charges

Order Value Shipping Charge
Below ₹499 ₹60 flat
₹499 and above FREE shipping

Shipping charges are calculated and displayed at checkout before payment.

4. Delivery Coverage

We currently ship to all states and union territories across India. We do not offer international shipping at this time.

If your pincode is not serviceable by our courier partner, we will contact you within 24 hours of order placement to discuss an alternative arrangement or offer a full refund.

5. Packaging

All Mom's Mix products are packed securely to ensure they arrive undamaged. We use food-safe packaging materials to maintain product freshness throughout transit.

6. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we will share the tracking details via WhatsApp or SMS. You can use the tracking number on the courier partner's website to monitor your delivery status.

For any tracking issues, feel free to WhatsApp us at +91 77022 60614.

7. Failed Delivery Attempts

If a delivery attempt fails due to an incorrect address or recipient unavailability, the courier will typically attempt delivery 1–2 more times. After that, the package may be returned to us. In such cases, re-shipping charges will apply.

Please ensure your delivery address and phone number are correct at the time of ordering.
