How to Prepare

Simple Steps, Authentic Taste

All our premixes follow a simple mix-and-ferment process — true to the traditional way.

🍚

Idli Premix

1

Take 1 measure of Mom's Mix Idli Mix.

2

Add 1¼ measures of water.

3

Add salt to taste and mix into a smooth batter.

4

Allow batter to ferment overnight or for 10–12 hours.

🥞

Dosa Premix

1

Take 1 measure of Mom's Mix Dosa Mix.

2

Add 1½ measures of water.

3

Add salt to taste and make a smooth batter.

4

Allow batter to ferment overnight or for 10–12 hours.

🫓

Dibba Rotte Premix

1

Take 1 measure of Mom's Mix Dibba Rotte Mix.

2

Add 1 measure of water.

3

Add salt to taste and mix into a smooth batter.

4

Allow batter to ferment overnight or for 10–12 hours.
