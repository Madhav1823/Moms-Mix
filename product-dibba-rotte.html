  1. Take 1 measure of Mom's Mix Dibba Rotte Mix.
  2. Add 1 measure of water.
  3. Add salt to taste and mix into a smooth batter.
  4. Allow the batter to ferment overnight or for 10–12 hours.
  5. Pour a thick layer onto a hot, oiled tawa. Cook covered on medium-low until the bottom is golden and the top is set, then flip to crisp the other side.

💡 Tip: Dibba Rotte is thicker than a dosa — use a heavy-bottomed pan and cook low and slow for an even crisp throughout.
