Soft Idli

Steamed

Classic Idli

Pillowy, steamed rice cakes — the quintessential South Indian breakfast. Light, healthy, and endlessly comforting.

12–15Yield
10 minPrep
12 minCook
10–12 hrsFermentation
Buy Idli Premix — ₹250 Full Prep Guide

Ingredients

  • 1 measure Mom's Mix Idli Premix
  • 1¼ measures water
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil or ghee for greasing moulds

Serve with: Coconut chutney, sambar, tomato chutney, or pickle.

Method

  1. 1
    Combine the premix with 1¼ measures of water and salt. Mix into a smooth batter.
  2. 2
    Ferment overnight or 10–12 hours in a warm spot.
  3. 3
    Lightly grease idli moulds with oil.
  4. 4
    Pour batter into moulds (fill ¾ full) and steam for 10–12 minutes.
  5. 5
    Rest for 2 minutes, then unmould with a wet spoon. Serve immediately.
🍽️ Serving Suggestion: Serve idlis hot with coconut chutney and a bowl of sambar. A smear of ghee on top adds wonderful flavour.

Crispy Dosa

Pan-fried

Classic Dosa

Thin, lacey, golden crepes with a satisfying crunch. One of the world's most beloved fermented foods.

6–8Yield
10 minPrep
20 minCook
10–12 hrsFermentation
Buy Dosa Premix — ₹220 Full Prep Guide

Ingredients

  • 1 measure Mom's Mix Dosa Premix
  • 1½ measures water
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for cooking

Optional: Potato masala filling for masala dosa, or chopped onion for onion dosa.

Method

  1. 1
    Mix premix with 1½ measures water and salt. Ferment 10–12 hours.
  2. 2
    Heat a tawa on medium-high until very hot. Sprinkle water — it should evaporate immediately.
  3. 3
    Pour a ladleful of batter and quickly spread in thin circles from the centre out.
  4. 4
    Drizzle oil around the edges. Cook until the base is golden and the edges lift.
  5. 5
    Fold into half or roll, and serve immediately.
🍽️ Serving Suggestion: Serve with coconut chutney, sambar, and tomato-onion chutney. For masala dosa, add spiced potato filling before folding.

Dibba Rotte

Telugu Tradition

Dibba Rotte

The beloved thick Telugu pan cake — uniquely crispy outside and soft-spongy inside. A comfort food like no other.

4–6Yield
10 minPrep
25 minCook
10–12 hrsFermentation
Buy Dibba Rotte Premix — ₹250 Full Prep Guide

Ingredients

  • 1 measure Mom's Mix Dibba Rotte Premix
  • 1 measure water
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil or ghee for cooking

Pairs beautifully with: Gongura pickle, coconut chutney, yoghurt, or avakaya (mango pickle).

Method

  1. 1
    Mix premix with equal water and salt. Ferment overnight 10–12 hours.
  2. 2
    Heat a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat. Add a generous drizzle of oil or ghee.
  3. 3
    Pour a thick layer of batter (about 1.5–2 cm). Cover and cook on medium-low heat.
  4. 4
    Cook until the bottom is deeply golden and the top is set (approx. 12–15 minutes).
  5. 5
    Flip carefully and cook the other side for 5–7 minutes until equally golden. Serve hot.
🍽️ Serving Suggestion: Traditionally served with gongura chutney or avakaya pickle and a side of yoghurt. Also pairs well with sambar.

Punugulu

Deep-fried

Punugulu

Andhra's beloved street-food fritters — crispy, golden, and addictive. One mix, three possible dishes.

20–24Pieces
10 minPrep
20 minCook
NoneFermentation
Buy Punugulu Premix — ₹220 Full Prep Guide

Ingredients

  • 1 measure Mom's Mix Punugulu Premix
  • 1 measure water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 green chillies, finely chopped
  • Oil for deep frying

Optional additions: curry leaves, ginger, coriander.

Method

  1. 1
    Combine premix, water, salt, onion, and green chillies into a thick batter.
  2. 2
    Heat oil in a deep pan to medium-high. Test with a small drop of batter — it should rise quickly.
  3. 3
    Drop spoonfuls of batter into the hot oil carefully.
  4. 4
    Fry until golden brown all over, turning as needed. Drain on paper towel.
  5. 5
    Serve hot immediately with coconut chutney.
🍽️ Serving Suggestion: Best eaten fresh and hot with coconut chutney or tamarind chutney. For uttapam or gunta pongannalu variation, see the Preparation Guide.

Gare

Festive Favourite

Gare (Medu Vada)

Fragrant, savory lentil fritters — the must-have at South Indian festivals, temple prasadams, and family feasts.

15–20Pieces
15 minPrep
25 minCook
2 hrsRest Time
Buy Gare Premix — ₹280 Full Prep Guide

Ingredients

  • 1 measure Mom's Mix Gare Premix
  • 1 measure water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2–3 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • Handful of fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Oil for deep frying

Method

  1. 1
    Mix premix with water, salt, ginger, chilli, onion, and coriander into a thick batter.
  2. 2
    Rest the batter for 2 hours before shaping.
  3. 3
    Heat oil to medium. Wet hands to prevent sticking.
  4. 4
    Take a portion, flatten into a disc and make a hole in the centre. Slide carefully into the oil.
  5. 5
    Fry on medium heat, turning once, until golden brown and crispy on both sides.
  6. 6
    Drain and serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
🍽️ Serving Suggestion: The classic combination is gare + sambar + coconut chutney. Also wonderful as a festive snack on its own, or dunked in sambar.
