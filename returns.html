Returns & Refunds

Our policy on returns, replacements, and refunds — straightforward and fair.

Last Updated: October 2026  ·  Return Window: 7 days from delivery  ·  Contact: momsmixtraditions@gmail.com

We stand behind the quality of every Mom's Mix product. If something isn't right with your order, we'll do our best to make it right — quickly and without hassle.

1. Return Eligibility

✅ We accept returns / replacements for:

  • Products that arrived damaged or broken during transit
  • Wrong product delivered (different from what was ordered)
  • Products with a manufacturing defect or quality issue
  • Packages that arrived tampered with or unsealed
  • Products delivered past their expiry date

❌ We do not accept returns for:

  • Change of mind after the order has been dispatched
  • Products that have been opened and partially used
  • Incorrect address provided at the time of ordering
  • Damage caused by improper storage after delivery
  • Products returned without prior intimation to us

2. Return Window

All return or replacement requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery. Requests made after this period may not be accepted.

Since our products are food items, we encourage you to check your order upon delivery and contact us immediately if there is any issue.

3. How to Raise a Return / Refund Request

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
    WhatsApp us at +91 77022 60614 or email momsmixtraditions@gmail.com with your order details.
  • Share photos or a short video
    Send clear photos of the damaged/incorrect product and the packaging so we can verify the issue quickly.
  • We'll review and respond within 24–48 hours
    Our team will assess your request and confirm whether a replacement or refund is applicable.
  • Replacement dispatched or refund initiated
    Approved replacements are dispatched within 2–3 business days. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

4. Refund Method & Timeline

Refunds are credited back to the original payment method used at the time of purchase:

  • UPI / Net Banking / Debit Card: 3–5 business days
  • Credit Card: 5–7 business days (depending on your bank)
  • Cash on Delivery orders: Refunded via bank transfer — please share your account details when raising the request

You will receive a confirmation message once the refund has been initiated from our end.

5. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch only. Once an order has been packed and handed over to the courier, it cannot be cancelled.

To cancel an order, contact us immediately via WhatsApp at +91 77022 60614. If the cancellation is accepted, a full refund will be processed within 3–5 business days.

6. Perishable Nature of Products

Our premixes are made without preservatives and have a limited shelf life. We strongly recommend checking the best-before date on the packaging upon delivery. If you receive a product close to or past its expiry, please contact us immediately — we will replace or refund it with no questions asked.
