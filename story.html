Our Mission

Traditional Indian Breakfasts, Made Convenient

Indian breakfast traditions are deeply tied to family, ritual, and region. A plate of soft idlis on a Sunday morning, a crispy dosa for a quick weekday breakfast, Dibba Rotte at a family gathering — these aren't just meals, they're memories. We set out to make it easier for every Indian household to prepare these beloved dishes without losing an ounce of their traditional character.

Our premixes start with split black gram that is soaked, cleaned, and dehusked the old-fashioned way — because shortcuts show up in the taste. There are no preservatives, no artificial colours, and no flavour shortcuts. Just honest ingredients prepared carefully, packaged so they're ready whenever you are.
