  1. Mix 1 measure of Mom's Mix Gare Mix with 1 measure of water.
  2. Add salt, ginger, green chilli, chopped onions, and coriander leaves.
  3. Use the batter after 2 hours (no overnight fermentation needed).
  4. Wet your hands, shape the batter into rings, and deep fry in hot oil.
  5. Fry on medium heat until golden brown and crispy. Drain and serve with coconut chutney and sambar.

💡 Tip: Gare batter should be thick enough to hold its shape. If it's too loose, let it rest a little longer before shaping.
